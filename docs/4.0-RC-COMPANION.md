# 4.0 — RCLootCouncil companion

Maintainer's call, 2026-08-17. KART stops shipping a loot council. RCLootCouncil owns
session, group loot, voting, trade and history. KART keeps raid-lead tools and adds two
hooks into RC: nickname-stable council membership, and council-driven award while the
raid leader still trades.

This document replaces `docs/OWNERSHIP.md` for loot. Invite, auto-promote and buff-check
ownership is unchanged.

## Why

The built-in LC exists because two things RC does not do: (1) fill config with Northern
Sky nicknames so an alt swap does not need a recouncil, and (2) let council members click
Award so the raid-lead tank is not stuck in the voting frame. A designated lootmaster who
is not the raid leader is **not** in scope — the lead keeps the items and the TradeUI.

WowUtils already paints crests, wishlist and sim gain onto RC's voting frame. ExtraUtilities
and GroupGear do the same job for their columns. KART will not duplicate that.

The desktop **[KART Companion](https://github.com/Kandera/KART-Companion)** tray app exists only
to write that sim cache (`KART_WoWUtilsCache`) because a WoW addon cannot fetch WowUtils itself.
4.0 does not ship it, does not require it, and does not intercept WowUtils addon comms
(`LootCouncilWU.lua` / in-KART droptimizer relay from the 3.5 line is void — do not merge).
The raid gets RCLootCouncil and the WowUtils addon together; those columns are WowUtils'
job. The C# Companion/Bridge stays maintainer-only. KART's in-addon **paste-import of raid
compositions** (Invite tab) is unrelated and stays.

## The companion is part of KART

Not a second addon. `RCCompanion.lua` loads from `KeineAhnungRaidTools.toc` with
`OptionalDeps: RCLootCouncil`. Namespace: `KART.RC`.

- RC present: companion runs. Own LC files are gone in 4.0, so there is no parallel
  session, no second Auto-Pass, no second Force-Win.
- RC absent: KART still does invites, promote and buffs. Once per login it prints that
  loot council needs RCLootCouncil. Blizzard's group loot is unmodified.

Everyone who should click Award, and the raid leader who receives those clicks, need
KART. The rest of the raid needs RC and the WowUtils addon (distributed with RC). NSRT is
still OptionalDeps — nicknames degrade to character names when it is missing, same as
today's `Identity.GetNickname`.

## What KART still owns

| Piece | 4.0 |
|---|---|
| Invites, auto-promote, buff-check, profiles, raid-lead bar | Unchanged |
| `KASC.Identity` (NSAPI nick ↔ GUID, `KART_PlayerCache`) | Unchanged |
| Council **names** the lead types (nick or character) | `KART_Settings.rcCouncilMembers`, migrated from `lcCouncilMembers` |
| Pushing those names into RC as GUIDs | Companion, raid leader only |
| Award click from a council client | Companion whispers the lead; lead's client calls RC's `Award()` |
| Nicknames in RC's voting/history/trade rows | Companion display hook, local |

## What RC owns (KART does not reimplement)

Session start/end, Auto Group Loot, `mldb`, vote buttons, min quality, rolls, TradeUI,
loot history, `/rc add`, item routing into the lead's bags. The raid leader is RC's
master looter. That is accepted.

## Nicknames — person follows the nick

RC stores `db.profile.council` as character GUIDs. A GUID is one alt. The companion
does not store GUIDs as the source of truth.

Source of truth: the lead's `rcCouncilMembers` string, same shape as today's council
field (comma/space separated nicks or names). On `GROUP_ROSTER_UPDATE` and when the
field is edited, if we are `UnitIsGroupLeader`:

1. Split the field, `KASC.Identity.ResolvePlayer` each token.
2. Keep only resolved `Player-*` keys for people actually in the group
   (`Identity.FindUnitForKey`).
3. Write that GUID list into RC's `db.profile.council`.
4. Call RC's council send (`RCLootCouncilML:SendCouncil` / `UpdateGroupCouncil` —
   use whichever the loaded RC version exposes; wrap in `pcall`).

A nick whose current alt is not in the raid is omitted until they join, then the next
roster pass inserts them. Stale GUIDs from last week's alt are not kept. Non-lead
clients never write RC's council table.

Display (every KART client): hook RC voting-frame name cells (and history/trade if the
same helper is one line) through `Identity.GetNickname` / `NSAPI:GetName`. Config is
not display. Display must not write SavedVariables.

## Award — council clicks, lead's client awards, lead trades

RC's voting-frame right-click menu returns immediately unless `addon.isMasterLooter`.
`RCLootCouncilML:Award` runs only on the ML client. Do not fake `isMasterLooter`.

- If we **are** ML: leave RC's native Award path alone.
- If we are **council and not ML**: show the award entries; on confirm, send
  `RC_AWARD` via `KASC:Send` as a **whisper to the ML's unit name**, payload
  `session:winnerName:responseID` (reason empty in v1). Refuse while addon
  restrictions are active (Midnight encounter lock), print once.
- On the ML client, `KASC:RegisterMessage("RC_AWARD", { whisper = true })`: require
  `Identity` of the sender to be in the current RC council GUID list (or our resolved
  `rcCouncilMembers` list); then `pcall` `RCLootCouncilML:Award`. RC broadcasts
  `awarded` and TradeUI opens on the lead, who holds the items.

Two council members clicking the same session: RC already supports re-award. No extra
lock. Do not send `awarded` ourselves.

## Explicitly out of scope (do not build)

- Designated lootmaster ≠ raid leader
- Own session, Force-Win, Auto-Pass, vote buttons, trade list, loot history
- Officer notes (ExtraUtilities guild-note column exists)
- WowUtils / Droptimizer / `KART_WoWUtilsCache` / `wowutils_data` / in-KART WU comm intercept
- The KART Companion tray app (C#) as a raid install. The maintainer may keep the Bridge locally.
- Forking RC or Reloe changing NSRT

If a later request wants any of those back, that is a new spec, not a 4.0 bug.
